Summary
======
I am a PhD candidate in statistical machine learning at Imperial College London, specialising in foundational reinforcement learning. My research develops efficient, theoretically grounded algorithms for sequential decision-making.

My interests include machine learning, reinforcement learning, transformers and LLMs, reasoning, statistics and probability, time series, privacy, ranking and recommendation, optimisation, and statistical modelling.

Education
======
* **PhD in Statistical Machine Learning**, Imperial College London, October 2022 – Present
  * Awarded the Roth Scholarship.
  * Supervised by Dr. Ciara Pike-Burke and Prof. Niall Adams.
  * Research focus includes reinforcement learning theory, multi-armed bandits, sequential testing for reinforcement learning, Bayesian optimisation, and LLM post-training using RLHF.
  * Also explored connections with change point detection, time series analysis, differential privacy, and ranking.
  * Expected completion: April 2027.

* **MSci in Mathematics**, Imperial College London, October 2018 – June 2022
  * First Class Honours.
