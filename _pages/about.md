I am a PhD candidate in the Statistics Section of the Department of Mathematics at Imperial College London, supervised by Dr. Ciara Pike-Burke and Prof. Niall Adams.

My research focuses on developing efficient reinforcement learning algorithms with strong performance guarantees. I am particularly interested in sequential decision-making, multi-armed bandits, active learning, Bayesian optimisation, preference feedback, RLHF, and LLM alignment, reasoning and orchestration.

In early 2026 I completed a six-month research internship at MediaTek Research, supervised by Dr. Sattar Vakili. During the internship, I worked on reinforcement learning from human feedback from both theoretical and practical perspectives, including Bayesian optimisation from preference feedback and efficient methods for LLM alignment.

I have recently joined Spotify this summer as a Research Intern here in London! I will be researching areas broadly around content understanding.
